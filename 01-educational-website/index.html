About Meridian

A teaching institute built around close mentorship

Founded in 1994, Meridian Institute grew from a single evening-college campus into a full university offering undergraduate, graduate and continuing-education programs. Our defining choice, then and now, is class size: no lecture hall seats more than sixty students.

That constraint shapes everything else — faculty who know your name, project work reviewed line by line, and career guidance that starts in your first term rather than your last.

  • Small cohort sizes across every program, undergraduate through doctoral
  • Industry-embedded capstone projects with partner organizations
  • Need-based and merit scholarships covering up to 100% of tuition
  • On-campus research labs open to undergraduates from year one
