About Cedarbrook

Twenty-six years of treating patients as people first

Cedarbrook Health opened in 2000 as a single outpatient clinic and has grown into a 180-bed hospital with a full diagnostic laboratory, without losing the thing patients say they value most: doctors who take the time to explain what's happening and why.
